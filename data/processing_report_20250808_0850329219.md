# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a Service Level Agreement (SLA) between Orange and its customers for the "Internet Direct Service" provided with managed routers. The SLA outlines the specific service standards Orange commits to meet for the availability of its network and associated router equipment. It details how service levels are measured, including "Site Availability" (SA) and "Router Availability" (RA), and defines various terms related to the service, such as customer premises equipment (CPE), tail circuits, and nodes. The document also specifies exclusions and conditions for the SLA, including circumstances that might void service credits or termination rights.

The main purpose of this SLA is to establish clear expectations for service performance and to provide customers with remedies, primarily in the form of service credits, if Orange fails to meet these agreed-upon levels. Key themes include network uptime, the impact of different site topologies (single versus dual router/access) on availability targets, and geographical variations in service levels across different regions. It also addresses the maximum time to attend (MTTA) for technical support under specific conditions. The information is valuable for customers as it quantifies the expected performance of the internet service and provides a framework for accountability and compensation in case of service degradation.

## Products & Services

*   **Product/Service Name:** Orange Internet Direct Service (for Service with Orange - Managed Routers) (mentioned in document)
*   **Service Offerings and Characteristics:**
    *   Provides reliable internet connectivity managed via Orange's network and routers. (mentioned in document)
    *   Includes managed routers (CPE) at customer premises. (mentioned in document)
    *   Offers options for enhanced resilience like "Dual Access" for diverse routing and no single point of failure. (mentioned in document)
    *   Service Level Agreements (SLAs) define performance metrics and remedies for non-compliance. (mentioned in document)
*   **Product Features and Specifications:**
    *   **Site Availability (SA):** Measures virtual communication link availability between a Location and the Orange Node.
        *   Single Router topology: SA = RA
        *   Dual router / leased line topology: SA = RA, but the site is not available only when both routers are unavailable during the same 5-minute intervals. (mentioned in document)
    *   **Router Availability (RA):** Measures the uptime of the router and its access link to the Orange CPE, excluding scheduled or customer-initiated maintenance. Expressed as a percentage of up-time vs. total time for the observation period. (mentioned in document)
    *   **Back-Up:** Redundant Tail Circuit with automatic switching capability on a separate circuit path. For hub/host locations, must include equivalent service levels and diverse routing. (mentioned in document)
*   **Pricing Information:**
    *   Credits are against "Qualifying Charges," which include all monthly recurring charges for the Service, excluding Tail Circuit Charges and one-time charges (installation, project management, professional services). (mentioned in document)
    *   The Service Level for Orange Service Delivery is only applicable if the Service implementation fee is charged to and paid by the Customer. (mentioned in document)
*   **Product Categories and Variants:**
    *   **Site Topologies:**
        *   Single Router (single Tail circuit)
        *   Dual Access (single router, dual Tail circuits & Nodes)
        *   Dual Access (dual routers, dual Tail circuits & Nodes) (mentioned in document)
    *   **Regional Variants:** Service Levels for Site Availability vary by region (Region A, B, C, D, E), with specific country assignments in Exhibit A. (mentioned in document)
