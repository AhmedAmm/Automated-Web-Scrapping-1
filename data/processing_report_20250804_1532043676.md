# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document outlines INRY's "SMART Success for Employee Center Pro" offering, a packaged service designed to help organizations transition to ServiceNow's modern Employee Center. The core purpose is to enhance employee engagement and self-service capabilities by migrating from legacy systems within a rapid 8-10 week timeframe. The solution leverages ServiceNow's Generative AI features, specifically Now Assist, for improved search and conversational interactions, aiming to create more informed and empowered employees.

The value proposition for businesses lies in INRY's expertise, rapid time-to-value, and flexible partnering models. INRY emphasizes their "SMART Success" approach, which includes a proven methodology, an experienced team, and post-go-live support through their CloudCover service for 90 days. This offering is positioned to deliver tangible business outcomes across employee experience, HR efficiency, IT integration, and compliance by standardizing secure HR processes and reducing risk.

Beyond the initial migration, the document details INRY's "CloudCover" managed services, which provide ongoing support, platform enhancements, operational maintenance, and strategic reviews. This extended support aims to ensure platform reliability, alignment with ServiceNow roadmaps, and continuous improvement of the employee experience, making it a comprehensive solution for maximizing the value of the ServiceNow Employee Center.
