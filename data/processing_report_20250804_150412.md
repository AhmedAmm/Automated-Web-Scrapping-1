# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing how Blue Diamond Growers, a major almond marketer and processor, successfully migrated its critical SAP infrastructure to Google Cloud with the assistance of Protera. The core challenge faced by Blue Diamond was instability and escalating, opaque costs from their previous private cloud provider, necessitating a swift relocation and a partner with deep cloud and SAP expertise.

The partnership with Protera enabled Blue Diamond Growers to migrate 38 SAP and SAP S/4HANA systems to Google Cloud within a compressed eight-week timeline. This move resulted in immediate benefits, including enhanced system stability, proactive monitoring through Protera Watchdog, and a significant 25% reduction in total cost of ownership (TCO). The case study highlights Protera's technical proficiency and managed services as key factors in achieving a smooth transition, delivering cost savings, and improving operational efficiency.

The overall purpose of this case study is to demonstrate the value Protera brings to complex cloud migrations for enterprise clients, particularly those running SAP workloads. The main business insights revolve around achieving stability, significant cost savings, and gaining transparency in cloud operations through expert partnership. For Blue Diamond Growers, this migration has positioned them for future growth and technological advancements, allowing their internal IT team to focus on strategic initiatives rather than infrastructure management.
