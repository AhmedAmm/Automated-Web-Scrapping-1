# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing how Exotic Metals Forming, a division of Parker Hannifin Aerospace, implemented ShopVue's Manufacturing Execution System (MES) to overcome significant operational challenges and achieve exponential growth. The aerospace manufacturer was struggling with an underdeveloped workforce, high employee turnover due to a competitive talent market, and outdated, paper-reliant shop floor systems. This led to inefficiencies, increased overtime, and difficulties in recruiting and retaining skilled labor.

The implementation of ShopVue's comprehensive MES solution, including modules for time and attendance, shop activity, machine interfacing, sequencing, and management reporting, proved transformative. Exotic Metals Forming achieved a remarkable 20:1 return on investment, a 25% reduction in overtime, and millions in annual savings. Key improvements include significantly enhanced labor efficiency, as operators spend less time on reporting and more on value-added tasks, and higher machine utilization through better scheduling and real-time monitoring. The MES solution also streamlined onboarding for new employees and provided crucial real-time visibility into production, enabling better management and control.

The value of this information lies in demonstrating the tangible benefits of a modern MES for a complex manufacturing environment like aerospace. It highlights how investing in technology can directly address challenges in labor management and operational efficiency, leading to substantial cost savings and improved profitability. The case study serves as a strong testament to ShopVue's effectiveness, particularly in its ability to improve both employee and machine utilization in a high-demand industry.
