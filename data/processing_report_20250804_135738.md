# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is an executive summary of a commissioned Forrester Consulting Total Economic Impact™ (TEI) study, conducted on behalf of AWS. It presents an analysis of the benefits derived from implementing AWS Cloud Operations, which are designed to optimize IT by improving management, security, and operations. These improvements lead to faster environment creation and application development, ultimately contributing to both cost savings and increased revenue through enhanced user experience and accelerated time-to-market.

The study reveals significant quantifiable benefits achieved by a composite organization utilizing AWS Cloud Operations over a three-year period. Key findings include an impressive 241% return on investment (ROI), $3.1 million saved due to faster deployment of workloads to production, and the avoidance of $7.8 million in lost revenue by significantly reducing downtime. Additionally, improved workload management through AWS services utilization generated $3.4 million in cost savings. The overall purpose of this information is to provide a compelling, data-driven business case for adopting AWS Cloud Operations, highlighting its potential to deliver substantial financial returns and operational efficiencies.
