# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a compilation of highly negative customer reviews for Orange España, primarily found on Trustpilot, where the company is rated as "Bad" with a score of 1.1 out of 5. The reviews span several months in 2025 and highlight severe issues with Orange's customer service, billing practices, and service quality.

Key themes include allegations of scamming customers through hidden charges and misleading contract terms, particularly affecting foreigners. Customers report excessive and unhelpful customer service interactions, including rudeness, hang-ups, and an inability to resolve issues. Service problems mentioned are poor internet speeds (even on 5G), unstable connections, and difficulties with account management, website usability, and AI chat support. The overarching sentiment is one of deep dissatisfaction and a strong warning for potential customers to avoid Orange España due to what is perceived as dishonesty and incompetence.
