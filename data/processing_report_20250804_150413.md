# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a white paper or article discussing the advantages of adopting cloud-based Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) in the modern manufacturing industry. It highlights that traditional on-premises MES solutions are becoming outdated due to evolving needs for cybersecurity, new capabilities, and agile operations. The core argument is that cloud-based MES, like GE Vernova's Proficy Smart Factory offerings, presents a superior alternative by simplifying implementation, reducing maintenance, and enhancing security.

The main business insight is that transitioning to cloud MES can lead to significant cost reductions, potentially up to 30%, by lowering capital and operating expenses and decreasing the need for internal IT resources. Furthermore, having Operational Technology (OT) data in the cloud unlocks powerful analytics for revealing hidden opportunities, boosting operational efficiency, increasing speed and agility, and providing critical intelligence for better decision-making across all levels of an organization. This shift enables manufacturers of all sizes to achieve digital transformation, foster continuous improvement, and gain a competitive edge.
