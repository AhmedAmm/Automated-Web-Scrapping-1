# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is an analyst report from GlobalData titled "The Evolution of Orange Business: From Connectivity to Digital Solutions," published on August 15, 2024. It assesses Orange Business's strategic transformation and performance, particularly in the Asia Pacific (APAC) region. The report highlights Orange Business as a carrier successfully evolving beyond its traditional connectivity services to compete effectively with system integrators and IT managed services providers in areas like cloud modernization, IoT, and customer/employee experience solutions.

Key insights indicate Orange Business is actively pursuing growth in APAC through digital services, with significant opportunities identified in markets like ASEAN, China, and India, despite global economic uncertainties. The company is noted for its progress in transforming legacy communication products into modern business solutions addressing experience requirements and is leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) to enhance its offerings and improve customer productivity. The report also points to substantial potential for Orange Business to gain market share in the digital infrastructure layer, supported by its Evolution Platform and cybersecurity capabilities. The overall value of the information lies in its analysis of Orange Business's successful adaptation to the digital landscape and its strong, committed presence in the dynamic APAC market.
