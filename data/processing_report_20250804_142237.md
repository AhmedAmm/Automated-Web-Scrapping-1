# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document details how BPX Energy, BP's U.S. onshore oil and gas production business, is transforming its production operations through industrial automation, specifically leveraging Amazon Web Services (AWS). The primary challenge identified was the need for real-time, granular data access for operational teams and machine learning solutions, which their previous data warehouse couldn't provide. This limited timely visibility into asset performance and hindered advanced analysis.

To address this, BPX Energy implemented an AWS-based solution, utilizing EOT's TwinTalk software and the Industrial Data Lake (IDL) with a Lake House architecture. This solution consolidates data from Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) assets, unlocks information from proprietary applications and data silos, and integrates Operational Technology (OT) with Information Technology (IT). Key benefits realized include near real-time data delivery from SCADA systems to AWS services like AWS IoT SiteWise and Amazon S3, enabling high-frequency analysis for critical use cases like wellhead pressure anomaly detection.

The overall purpose of this initiative is to enhance business value by democratizing data, increasing operational efficiency, and reducing costs. BPX Energy has achieved a significant 45% reduction in IT and OT Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), demonstrating the tangible business impact of this strategic cloud adoption for improved operational intelligence and decision-making.
