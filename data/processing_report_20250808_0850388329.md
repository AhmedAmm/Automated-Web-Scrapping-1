# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document announces an expanded partnership between Orange and Google Cloud, focused on deploying artificial intelligence (AI) and generative AI (GenAI) across Orange's global operations and customer touchpoints. Leveraging Google Distributed Cloud (GDC), Orange aims to bring AI capabilities closer to its data centers and customers, enabling compliance with local data residency requirements and accelerating AI adoption.

The collaboration targets three key areas: enhancing network performance through AI-driven automation for tasks like capacity planning and root cause analysis; improving operational efficiency and customer service by enabling faster access to information and quicker issue resolution via on-premise GenAI models; and elevating customer experience with personalized recommendations and localized AI-powered speech recognition. This strategic move is designed to unlock significant value from Orange's extensive data, fostering agility, responsiveness, and new business opportunities across its 26 operating countries.
