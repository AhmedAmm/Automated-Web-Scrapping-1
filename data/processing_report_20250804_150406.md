# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document outlines the comprehensive support services provided by IONOS Inc. to its Cloud customers and partners. For general Cloud customers, IONOS offers 24/7 technical support via hotline and email, with defined response times for malfunctions (under 1 hour) and service requests (under 72 hours). Support covers troubleshooting, maintenance announcements, service requests, account management, and incident status updates, with a clear responsibility that IONOS support will not log into or modify customer virtual resources.

The document details a tiered support structure for IONOS partners, differentiating between 1st, 2nd, and 3rd level support. 1st level support is the partner's responsibility for customer inquiries and basic issues, while escalating technical problems to IONOS. 2nd level support, achievable by "Platinum" partners and mandatory for Distributors, requires advanced technical qualification and detailed error analysis. IONOS exclusively provides 3rd level support for core IONOS Cloud services. The document emphasizes strict communication protocols, emphasizing that end customers do not have direct access to IONOS 24/7 Cloud Support, and outlines training and authentication requirements for partners to ensure efficient and secure support interactions.
