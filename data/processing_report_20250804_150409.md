# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing how East Branch Engineering and Manufacturing successfully leveraged ProShop, an ERP system, to overcome significant operational challenges and achieve substantial business growth. Initially struggling with their previous ERP system (E2 by Shoptech) due to limitations in managing paperless travelers, blanket orders, documentation, and quality control, East Branch sought a more robust solution. Their decision to implement ProShop, following extensive research, marked a turning point in their operations.

The implementation of ProShop led to dramatic improvements across East Branch's business. Key results include an 18% increase in revenue, a complete elimination of expediting fees for outside processes ($20,000 annual saving), and a $12,000 reduction in shipping fees. Furthermore, on-time delivery improved from 70% to 100% for their largest customer, and setup times were cut by over 50%. The system's capabilities enabled a shift to a more lean flow processing method and streamlined crucial functions like purchasing and quality management, significantly reducing administrative burdens and eliminating downtime.

The overarching theme of this case study is the transformative impact of ProShop on East Branch's efficiency, profitability, and ability to secure larger contracts. ProShop has become the "backbone" of their operations, enabling them to reduce costs, improve throughput, enhance organization, and instill customer confidence. The information presented holds significant value for manufacturing businesses looking to upgrade their ERP systems and achieve similar performance gains, highlighting ProShop's effectiveness in addressing common industry pain points.
