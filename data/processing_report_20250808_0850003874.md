## Document Overview
This document is a personal account detailing a user's consistently poor experience with Orange's internet service in a rural French village. The author, a photographer, highlights dissatisfaction with Orange's pricing and a recurring issue where internet speeds significantly degrade after approximately one week of use, despite the absence of local network congestion. This decline in performance forces repeated, unproductive interactions with Orange customer service and engineers, offering little to no resolution.

The core themes revolve around unreliable service, perceived speed throttling by Orange, and a significant disparity in value compared to services in the UK. The author's inability to suspend service while abroad and the high cost for slow speeds are major pain points. The information's business relevance lies in identifying potential customer dissatisfaction with a major telecommunications provider in a specific geographic region, suggesting potential issues with service quality, customer support effectiveness, and competitive pricing. This anecdotal evidence could be valuable for Orange to investigate their rural service performance and customer retention strategies.
