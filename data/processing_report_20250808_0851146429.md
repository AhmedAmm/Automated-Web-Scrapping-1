# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a market analysis report highlighting a significant shift in the global Ethernet provider landscape. The key takeaway is that **Colt has overtaken Orange Business** to become the top global Ethernet provider according to Vertical Systems Group's (VSG) year-end 2023 Global Provider Ethernet LEADERBOARD. This marks Colt's first time achieving this top ranking, a position previously held by Orange Business for two years.

The analysis attributes Colt's rise to strategic acquisitions, particularly of Lumen's assets in the EMEA region, which expanded its service reach and customer base. Orange Business, conversely, experienced a slip in ranking due to service transformations and customer migration. The report also notes the stable positions of Verizon and AT&T, the entry of Cirion into the leaderboard, and provides a list of companies in the "Challenge Tier." Despite low overall port growth globally, the market is seeing customers upgrade to higher-speed Ethernet services to support demanding applications like AI and cloud connectivity, with continued geographic expansion in the global Ethernet market.
