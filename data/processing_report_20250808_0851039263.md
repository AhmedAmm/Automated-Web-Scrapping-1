# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is an executive summary of Arcep's annual "Telconomics" progress report on the French telecommunications market, specifically focusing on 2023 data and upcoming regulatory directions. It highlights Arcep's workstreams, including the copper network switch-off, support for French businesses' digital transformation, promoting superfast network rollouts through initiatives like network sharing, addressing the environmental impact of digital technologies, and expanding regulatory responsibilities into the data and cloud economy as part of Europe's data strategy.

Key findings indicate that despite a 5.2% decrease to €13.8 billion in 2023, operator investments in electronic communications and mobile infrastructure remained strong, exceeding pre-2021 levels. Spending on superfast fixed and mobile deployments saw a decline, partly due to slower fiber rollout. However, fiber network sharing continues to grow, increasing choice for consumers, while mobile network sharing remains stable, particularly in rural areas. The nominal retail market income for operators rose by 2.5% in 2023, driven by a 5% increase in fixed broadband and superfast broadband services revenue, influenced by price adjustments. The mobile market also saw revenue growth in both services and handset sales, with a generally stable average monthly mobile bill despite some price increases for individual plans. As of the end of 2023, two-thirds of internet subscriptions were fiber-based (FTTH), and 17% of SIM cards were activated on 5G networks, accompanied by an 18% surge in monthly data traffic.
