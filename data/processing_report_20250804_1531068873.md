## Document Overview
This document outlines the comprehensive 24/7 Cloud Support services offered by IONOS Inc. to its Cloud customers, detailing available support channels, communication protocols, and response times for various request types. The overall purpose is to inform customers and partners about the support framework, response guarantees, and the responsibilities of each party within the IONOS Cloud ecosystem.
