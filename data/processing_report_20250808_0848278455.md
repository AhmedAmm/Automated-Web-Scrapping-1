# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document presents Orange's consolidated financial results for the first half of 2025, indicating a solid performance with significant growth in key metrics. The company reported a 3.8% increase in EBITDAaL (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization and Lease Costs) and a 7.7% rise in Organic Cash Flow from telecom activities. Notably, France experienced an acceleration in EBITDAaL growth, and the Africa & Middle East region demonstrated double-digit growth for the tenth consecutive half, driven by 4G/5G network expansion, Orange Money, and B2B services. Orange has also upgraded its full-year 2025 EBITDAaL growth guidance to above 3%.

The report highlights strong commercial performance across France, Europe, and Africa & Middle East, with continued leadership in convergence and mobile contracts. While overall revenues saw a slight increase, driven by retail services, Orange Business faced a decline, though Orange Cyberdefense showed positive momentum. The company is actively investing in its infrastructure, with a focus on FTTH, and is also pursuing strategic initiatives like a new defense and security division within Orange Business. Beyond financial performance, Orange is committed to sustainability, exceeding GHG emission reduction targets and advancing digital inclusion. The financial results are robust, supporting the company's commitment to a dividend floor and its medium-term financial objectives.
