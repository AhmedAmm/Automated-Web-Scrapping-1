## Document Overview
This document is a "Meet the Team" profile for Cloudtech, presenting the extensive backgrounds of its key founders: Tim Pullen, CEO and Founder, and Sinéad Leigh, Director and Joint Founder. Its primary purpose is to establish the company's credibility and expertise by showcasing the deep, complementary experience of its leadership in technology, business solutions, and client management.

The profiles highlight Cloudtech's strategic focus and evolution. Tim Pullen, with over forty years in IT and business (including roles at the Bank of England and Orange), founded Cloud Technology Ltd. (trading as Cloudtech) in 2009. Initially working with both Zoho and Salesforce.com, Cloudtech strategically narrowed its focus exclusively to the Zoho suite of business solutions from 2016, a decision that has led to the successful delivery of over 700 projects. Sinéad Leigh complements this technical and strategic depth with over fifteen years in B2B/B2C account management and direct marketing, having worked with numerous blue-chip clients such as HSBC and Vodafone. This demonstrates Cloudtech's strong foundation, proven track record, and a clear, specialized value proposition in implementing Zoho-centric cloud solutions, underpinned by experienced leadership in both technology and client relationship management.
