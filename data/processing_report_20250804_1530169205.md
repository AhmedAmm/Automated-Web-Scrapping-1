# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a comprehensive guide to the top cloud service providers (CSPs) globally in 2025. It begins by defining what a CSP is and the fundamental benefits they offer compared to building on-premises infrastructure, such as reduced capital expenditure, faster time-to-market, and enhanced agility. The content also addresses key challenges associated with using CSPs, including data confidentiality, security concerns, and the risk of vendor lock-in, highlighting the growing trend towards multi-cloud strategies to mitigate these issues.

The analysis delves into the market share of the dominant players, identifying Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) as the leading providers, collectively holding over 63% of the market. It provides a detailed overview of each of these top providers, along with other significant CSPs like Alibaba Cloud, IBM Cloud, DigitalOcean, and Salesforce, outlining their specific strengths, service offerings, and target markets. The document serves as a valuable resource for businesses seeking to understand the cloud computing landscape, evaluate different providers, and make informed decisions about their cloud adoption strategies, ultimately aiming to help users view, understand, and control their cloud costs effectively.
