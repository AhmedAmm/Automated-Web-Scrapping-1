# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a compilation of highly negative customer reviews for Orange Egypt, primarily focused on their mobile and internet services. The overwhelming sentiment across these reviews indicates severe dissatisfaction, with customers reporting instances of being scammed with data bundles, experiencing unreliable internet service, poor coverage, and a complete lack of responsive or accessible customer support. Many users describe feeling cheated, overcharged, and ignored when attempting to resolve issues.

The key themes highlight a perception of deliberate deception and poor business practices by Orange Egypt. Customers specifically mention being misled about data allowances, with purchased amounts significantly differing from what the app or network reports. The inability to reach customer service through any channel, including phone numbers that are reportedly disabled or lead to extensive wait times, is a recurring and significant complaint. These issues collectively suggest a company that prioritizes customer acquisition through potentially misleading tactics and offers minimal post-sale support, leading to extreme customer frustration and a strong recommendation to avoid their services.
