# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a report by Amazon Web Services (AWS) and The Hackett Group, summarizing a global study of 600 organizations, with a significant focus on industrial manufacturers, to assess the business impact of cloud adoption. The core of the report details how cloud migration translates into tangible improvements across key operational and strategic areas.

The study identifies four primary areas of business value: optimized manufacturing and IT operations leading to reduced downtime (39% and 36% respectively), enhanced supplier management with a 44% improvement in sourcing savings, increased sales efficiency and customer satisfaction (34% CSAT improvement through better digital collaboration), and accelerated business agility and innovation with a 36% improvement in time-to-market for organizations migrating the most applications. The information is highly valuable for industrial manufacturers seeking to understand and quantify the benefits of moving to the cloud, offering insights into effective adoption strategies and best practices.
