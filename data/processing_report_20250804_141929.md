# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document outlines the capabilities of **Unaric Reports**, a solution designed to enhance **Salesforce** reporting. It details how users can **format** Salesforce reports with custom branding, various data visualizations, and point-and-click editing. Furthermore, it explains the ability to **convert** Salesforce reports into multiple file formats such as PDF, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and CSV for flexible data sharing and analysis.

The core value proposition lies in enabling users to efficiently **send and schedule** these enhanced reports to both Salesforce and non-Salesforce stakeholders through various channels including email, DocuSign, Slack, and cloud storage services like Google Drive and Microsoft OneDrive. The scheduling feature allows for automated, recurring report delivery with intelligent skipping of empty reports and activity tracking. This streamlines workflows, improves collaboration, and ensures timely access to critical business insights, as evidenced by the reported generation of over a million reports monthly by their customers.
