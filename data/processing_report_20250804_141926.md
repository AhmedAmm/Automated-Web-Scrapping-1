# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is an announcement for a Forrester Consulting study commissioned by Plex (now part of Rockwell Automation) to evaluate the Total Economic Impact of the Plex Smart Manufacturing Platform. The study highlights significant financial and operational benefits for companies adopting the platform.

The core business insight is that the Plex Smart Manufacturing Platform delivers a substantial 412% return on investment over three years, with a rapid break-even point of under six months. Key benefits identified include reduced IT administration costs, improved efficiency through electronic quality checks, substantial savings from reduced inventory waste (an estimated $2.5 million), elimination of chargebacks due to production delays, and enhanced collaboration by breaking down silos between various departments like warehousing, manufacturing, maintenance, and customer service.

The overall purpose of this information is to showcase the quantifiable value and positive business outcomes achievable with the Plex Smart Manufacturing Platform. It serves as a powerful endorsement from a reputable third-party research firm, providing compelling data for potential customers to consider the platform's strategic and financial advantages for their manufacturing operations.
