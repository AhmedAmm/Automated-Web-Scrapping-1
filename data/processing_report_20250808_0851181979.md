# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document, titled "SD-WAN Supports Digital Transformation," highlights how Colgate-Palmolive partnered with Orange Business to implement a co-managed SD-WAN solution. This strategic move was driven by Colgate-Palmolive's ambition to achieve digital transformation, requiring a cost-effective, scalable, flexible, and secure global network. The core purpose of this information is to demonstrate the business value of SD-WAN in enabling a multinational corporation to support its digital initiatives, drive innovative growth, and meet stringent compliance requirements.

The main business insights revolve around how SD-WAN, by decoupling hardware from orchestration, significantly enhances network agility, improves performance, and centralizes control for a global infrastructure. Key themes include the critical role of reliable and secure connectivity in supporting data-driven strategies, e-commerce, and advanced analytics for Colgate-Palmolive's 34,000 employees across over 200 countries. The implementation, including a successful proof of concept, also addresses complexities like bandwidth management and the intelligent utilization of existing MPLS connections where necessary, ultimately reducing total cost of ownership and ensuring a future-ready network.
