# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document outlines Orange Group's robust financial results for the third quarter of 2024. It highlights key performance drivers, including significant growth in retail services across France and Europe, and exceptional performance in the Africa & Middle East region, driven by strong contributions from mobile data, fixed broadband, Orange Money, and B2B activities. The report also details revenue growth of 1.6% and EBITDAaL growth of 2.7%, confirming the successful execution of the "Lead the Future" strategic plan.

The insights reveal Orange's strengthened market leadership in France through a focused convergence strategy, and the significant impact of innovative platforms like "Max-It" in Africa & Middle East. Despite a decline in Europe due to wholesale services and a dip in Orange Business (impacted by fixed-only revenues), the Group's overall commercial momentum remains strong, particularly in mobile contracts and very high-speed fixed broadband. Crucially, Orange reaffirms its 2024 financial targets, including low single-digit growth in EBITDAaL and a commitment to disciplined eCAPEX, signaling continued financial health and strategic execution.
