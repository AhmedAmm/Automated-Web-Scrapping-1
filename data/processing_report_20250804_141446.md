# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a guide aimed at helping organizations navigate the complex compliance landscape of the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT). It outlines the critical role of compliance in securing operations and building stakeholder trust, especially in the face of an evolving regulatory environment driven by IIoT adoption. The guide emphasizes that understanding and adhering to key standards is paramount for market access and operational integrity.

The content details several essential compliance frameworks, including the EU Cybersecurity Act, NIST guidelines (U.S.), ENISA guidelines, the UK PSTI Act, GDPR, and ISO/IEC 27001. It then provides five actionable steps for achieving IIoT compliance: implementing robust access controls (mentioning Multi-Factor Authentication and Role-Based Access Control), prioritizing data encryption, conducting regular audits and risk assessments, managing legacy devices and patching, and maintaining comprehensive documentation. The guide specifically highlights DigiCert's Device Trust Manager and TrustCore SDK as solutions designed to assist companies in meeting these compliance requirements through enhanced device authentication, certificate management, and cryptographic capabilities.
