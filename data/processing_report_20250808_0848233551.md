# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a success story highlighting how Talan, a global consulting and technology group, significantly enhanced its financial visibility and project manager autonomy by implementing the Kantata Professional Services Cloud. Facing challenges with an outdated internal system and aggressive growth targets, Talan sought a scalable solution to manage its complex, multi-entity financial operations and project portfolios.

By adopting Kantata SX, Talan achieved key benefits including a 70% faster retrieval of KPIs and a 40% faster monthly close process, reducing closing times by several days. The implementation empowered project managers with greater autonomy and control over their projects, shifting responsibilities away from back-office support. Furthermore, Kantata streamlined intercompany billing and consolidated organizational data, providing real-time, accurate insights into project status and financial performance across all levels of management. This strategic move has not only improved operational efficiency but also supported Talan's rapid international expansion and financial growth.
