# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document, "Enterprise Connectivity Trends For 2025" by Expereo, is an industry analysis predicting key shifts in how businesses will manage their network infrastructure over the next year. The core theme is the increasing demand for agile, scalable, and secure networks, driven by budget uncertainties, technological advancements, and evolving business needs. The article emphasizes that successful enterprises will proactively adopt these trends to enhance their connectivity, collaboration, and security.

Key trends highlighted include the rise of Network as a Service (NaaS) due to budget flexibility, the adoption of next-gen Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi 6 & 7) for improved speed and capacity, and increased use of SD-WAN for cost savings and better management. Additionally, the analysis points to the growing maturity of LEO satellite connectivity, the importance of intelligent networking powered by AIOps for performance optimization and automation, and the critical need for comprehensive network visibility. The document's value lies in providing actionable insights for IT leaders to strategically plan and upgrade their enterprise connectivity solutions for optimal operational efficiency and future growth.
