# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing how ANZ Bank, a leading financial services institution in Australia and New Zealand, successfully accelerated its home loan origination process using Temporal, a code-first workflow orchestration tool. Faced with significant challenges in a year-long effort to digitally transform their home loan application process for their new ANZ Plus app, ANZ's engineering team discovered Temporal and found it could dramatically improve efficiency and system simplification.

The adoption of Temporal enabled ANZ to rebuild and deploy their home loan origination system in a matter of weeks, a stark contrast to the previous 18-month struggle. This solution not only ensured process durability, consistency, and compliance but also allowed ANZ to implement a domain-driven design, breaking down the loan system into composable microservices managed by Temporal Workflows. This architectural shift empowered engineers to focus on business logic, leading to faster feature delivery, improved customer experience, and enhanced system scalability and maintainability. The bank further benefited from migrating to Temporal Cloud, which streamlined operations and proved more cost-effective and resilient than their self-hosted solution.

The success of the home loan origination project has led to the widespread adoption of Temporal across ANZ, with over 500 engineers now utilizing it for critical workflows in areas such as payment processing, fraud detection, and Know Your Customer (KYC) initiatives. The case study highlights Temporal's value in enabling large organizations to build complex, agile, and scalable digital banking solutions rapidly, ultimately improving customer experience and driving business innovation.
