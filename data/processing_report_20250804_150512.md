# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a customer success story detailing how Imperial Brands PLC, a global consumer goods company with a heritage in tobacco products, successfully migrated its on-premise ERP system to the QAD Cloud. Imperial Brands, known for brands like Davidoff and Golden Virginia, faces challenges in a shifting market and sought to enhance efficiency, optimize costs, and increase operational flexibility.

The key insight is that by moving their ERP system to the QAD Cloud, Imperial Brands achieved significant benefits, including reduced risk, enhanced business continuity, and transformed variable costs into predictable ones. The migration, implemented across 21 facilities in 18 countries, was smooth with no disruption to daily operations or retraining required. This move allowed Imperial Brands' internal IT staff to focus on higher-value projects, provided 24/7 global support, guaranteed uptime, and strengthened data security. The overall value lies in demonstrating how QAD's cloud solution enabled Imperial Brands to overcome operational challenges and position itself for sustainable growth with greater stability and scalability.
