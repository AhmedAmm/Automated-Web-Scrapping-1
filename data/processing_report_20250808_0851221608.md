# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document, a blog post from Expereo titled "7 Challenges When Implementing a Network," highlights the complexities businesses face when establishing internet connectivity, particularly for global operations. Written by Frank de Bloois, President of Americas at Expereo, it details common pitfalls such as navigating international standardization, dealing with local permits and infrastructure limitations (even in metropolitan areas), and overcoming logistical hurdles with suppliers and equipment delivery. The piece also addresses the difficulties of post-implementation support across different regions and languages.

The core business insight is that while seemingly straightforward, network implementation is fraught with challenges that can significantly impact global expansion plans. Expereo positions itself as the solution provider, leveraging its 16 years of experience and extensive global network to overcome these issues. The article serves as a valuable resource for IT leaders and businesses contemplating or currently undertaking network deployment, underscoring the need for expert assistance to ensure smooth and reliable connectivity, allowing companies to focus on their core business.
