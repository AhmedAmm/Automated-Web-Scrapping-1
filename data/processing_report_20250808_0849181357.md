# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document outlines a significant shift in how the French State procures and manages IT services. It details a new circular, signed by Prime Minister Elisabeth Borne, aimed at regaining control over the increasing reliance on external IT service providers (ESNs) and consulting firms. The core objective is to enhance transparency, better manage outsourcing, and mitigate potential negative consequences, including high costs and risks associated with reduced internal expertise.

Key themes include the government's commitment to strengthening internal digital skills through recruitment and re-internalization initiatives, as highlighted by the role of the Interministerial Directorate for Digital (DINUM). The circular establishes stricter controls on the use of these external services, including a ban on outsourcing project piloting, mandatory annual reporting for strategic consulting, and ministerial-level oversight for technical services. Furthermore, measures are being implemented to prevent conflicts of interest and protect sensitive data. The document also briefly mentions the Disruptiv'Summit, organized by Républik-IT, which focuses on strategic IT procurement for IT Directors, suggesting an industry-wide dialogue on these critical issues.
