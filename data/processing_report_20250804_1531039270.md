# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document details the journey of a global food retailer and distributor to the public cloud, primarily leveraging Amazon Web Services (AWS). The initial impetus for this move was to consolidate fragmented systems following acquisitions, enhance data management, and improve the customer experience. While the migration to AWS initially yielded significant cost savings (a 50% reduction in footprint and a reported $50 million year-over-year saving), it also highlighted organizational and operational hurdles. These included siloed teams, a lack of automation, and a need for new cloud-specific skills.

To address these challenges and fully realize the benefits of cloud adoption, the company partnered with Deloitte to implement an Agile operating model. This transformation focused on fostering collaboration between development and operations, emphasizing automation and self-service capabilities. The new model empowered cross-functional product teams, such as Warehouse Management, to own their technology stack, leading to faster feature delivery (reduced from 50 to 19 days) and improved IT-business alignment. The overall value of this information lies in demonstrating how a large organization can overcome common cloud migration challenges by adopting a modern, agile approach, ultimately driving significant cost efficiencies and accelerating business outcomes.
