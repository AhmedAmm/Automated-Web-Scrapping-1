# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is an IT Executive Insight report from The Hackett Group, published in February 2023, analyzing the business impact of cloud adoption for industrial manufacturers. Based on a study of 600 organizations, it quantifies the benefits of cloud migration across four key areas: optimizing manufacturing operations, improving supplier management, enhancing sales efficiency and customer satisfaction, and increasing business agility and innovation. The report highlights significant improvements such as a 16% increase in overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), a 33% rise in sourcing savings, a 42% improvement in revenue per sales professional, and a 22% faster speed to market for new products.

The core insight is that cloud adoption is a critical enabler of digital transformation for industrial manufacturers, allowing them to navigate challenges like supply chain disruptions and rising costs more effectively. The Hackett Group's research demonstrates a direct correlation between cloud investment and improved business outcomes, with more extensive cloud adoption and strategic migration approaches yielding even greater benefits. The report provides actionable recommendations for organizations looking to maximize their cloud investment, emphasizing improved supplier management, customer engagement, operational resilience, and innovation.
