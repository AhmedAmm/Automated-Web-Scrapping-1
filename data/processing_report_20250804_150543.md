This document from Gartner Peer Insights provides a comprehensive review and rating of global Industrial IoT (IIoT) platforms, offering essential insights for businesses navigating this rapidly evolving technology landscape. It defines IIoT platforms as critical middleware that integrates operational technology (OT) assets and industrial data to enable intelligent applications, drive asset management decision-making, and provide operational visibility. Key use cases highlighted include automation augmentation, remote operations, sustainability, and IT/OT convergence.

The document lists and briefly describes prominent IIoT platform providers, including Cumulocity, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft, PTC, Siemens AG, Oracle, Braincube, IBM, Hitachi Vantara, Litmus, SAP, XCMG, Eurotech, ROOTCLOUD TECHNOLOGY, Altizon, Flutura, Exosite, and Davra. This information is valuable for enterprises seeking to understand the competitive landscape, identify potential partners, and make informed decisions about adopting IIoT solutions to enhance efficiency, innovation, and digital transformation.
