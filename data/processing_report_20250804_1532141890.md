# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing how a global chemical company partnered with Deloitte to modernize its enterprise integration platform and execute a complex cloud migration. The primary goal was to integrate SAP S/4HANA in the cloud, encompassing both IT and operational technologies across six continents, all within an aggressive two-year timeframe.

The company leveraged Deloitte's expertise to select and implement Microsoft Azure Integration Services, successfully deploying a robust, multi-region cloud platform. Key achievements include integrating diverse systems (on-premises, cloud, and trading partners), establishing clear processes, developing an IT/OT integration framework, and building internal skills for future growth. The project also emphasized resilience through automated failover and knowledge transfer, enabling the company to meet its demanding deadline and establish a foundation for ongoing cloud modernization. The value of this information lies in demonstrating a successful strategic partnership for digital transformation, highlighting efficient project execution, and showcasing tangible benefits like improved operational efficiency and future-proofing IT infrastructure.
