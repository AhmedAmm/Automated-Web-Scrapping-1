# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing how Kärcher, a global leader in cleaning technology, successfully launched a new, cloud-native manufacturing facility in Vietnam in February 2024. Partnering with AWS and its partner Zoi, Kärcher leveraged Amazon Web Services (AWS) to build the facility entirely on the cloud, avoiding significant upfront hardware and licensing costs and reducing infrastructure maintenance. This innovative approach allowed them to meet an aggressive 11-month timeline for project completion, which was crucial given recent global supply chain disruptions.

The key business insights highlight the significant advantages of a cloud-first strategy in modern manufacturing. Kärcher achieved a 100% reduction in infrastructure licensing and maintenance costs and optimized operating expenses by utilizing AWS services like Amazon EC2, Amazon RDS, and Amazon VPC. This project not only delivered a highly responsive and flexible production environment but also served as a proof of concept, demonstrating Kärcher's capability to fully transition its global operations to the cloud, with the Vietnam facility acting as a repeatable template for future expansions. The value of this information lies in showcasing a successful, rapid deployment of a modern, cost-effective, and scalable manufacturing operation through strategic cloud adoption.
