This document is a promotional piece for Dedicated Leased Lines, specifically highlighting a 10Gbps business internet service offered by Elevate. The content focuses on the benefits of a dedicated, unshared internet connection, emphasizing its security, reliability, and consistent performance for businesses. Key features include fixed, symmetrical bandwidth, a unique 100% uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA) through a dual fibre and rooftop wireless failover system, and rapid deployment times (as little as 10 days).

The service is positioned as the "last internet connection you'll ever need" due to its scalability, end-to-end network ownership and management by Elevate's in-house engineers, and its ability to eliminate lag and downtime. The value proposition centers on providing businesses with enhanced security, predictable performance, and uninterrupted operations, making it suitable for bandwidth-hungry, security-conscious, and multi-site organizations.
