# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing how ACME Manufacturing, a large, distributed manufacturer, successfully transformed its IT security and operational efficiency by implementing Agilicus AnyX. Faced with a complex and risky VPN-based infrastructure for accessing its critical SAP system across multiple divisions and contract manufacturers, ACME sought a more secure, efficient, and modern solution that wouldn't disrupt its core business operations. Key drivers for this transformation included increasing cybersecurity threats, evolving SEC disclosure requirements, and the need for enhanced operational agility.

The implementation of Agilicus AnyX enabled ACME Manufacturing to transition to a Zero Trust environment, significantly enhancing security through single-sign-on and multi-factor authentication for all users. This solution eliminated the costly and vulnerable VPN mesh, reduced the risk of cascading failures, and streamlined user access management. As a result, ACME achieved its objectives of increased security and efficiency, met compliance requirements for cyber insurance, and saw an unexpected benefit of improved real-time data access for executive management. The project was completed rapidly with minimal disruption, demonstrating the value of identity-aware access management in modernizing complex manufacturing IT environments.
