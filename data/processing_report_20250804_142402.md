## Business Operations & Workflow
- **Described Business Process:** The 8-step RFP process:
    1.  **Discovery:** Identify stakeholders and project needs, clarify scope, establish a timeline.
    2.  **Requirements Gathering:** Clearly list business, technical, and operational requirements, distinguishing "must-have" from "nice-to-have," and noting compliance standards.
    3.  **Creation:** Write the RFP document including executive summary, scope, requirements, timelines, budget, evaluation criteria, and submission instructions.
    4.  **Issuing the RFP:** Distribute to proven vendors with sufficient review time (2-4 weeks) and provide a contact point for questions.
    5.  **Vendor Comparison:** Evaluate proposals using a scoring system based on RFP criteria (cost, technical expertise, innovation, alignment). Gather stakeholder input.
    6.  **Shortlisting:** Narrow down candidates based on proposal content, reputation, past performance, and customer feedback. Ensure compliance and security standards are met.
    7.  **Demos and Feedback:** Request live demonstrations or trials, ask detailed questions, and gather structured team feedback.
    8.  **Selection and Contracting:** Choose the vendor offering the best value (not just lowest cost), negotiate a contract with clear deliverables, timelines, payment terms, performance metrics, and conflict resolution clauses.
- **Service Methodologies:** Structured, formal, and transparent.
- **Quality Control Measures:** Clearly defined requirements, evaluation criteria, scoring systems, and stakeholder feedback loops.
