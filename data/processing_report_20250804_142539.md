# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a case study detailing Harsco Rail's successful migration to Infor's CloudSuite Industrial Enterprise. The study highlights how this move has modernized Harsco Rail's operations by providing a unified system and access to advanced technology. Key achievements include a significant reduction in transaction processing time to 24-48 hours and the consolidation of data across 35 countries, all accomplished within a smaller IT footprint and a rapid 120-day deployment.

The primary business insight is the transformative power of Infor CloudSuite Industrial Enterprise as a digital backbone for industrial companies. It enables automation of complex tasks, improves customer engagement through product configuration and lifecycle management, integrates operations with industry best practices for project-based manufacturing, and enhances supply chain transparency. The overall purpose and value of this information lie in demonstrating how Infor's solution can drive efficiency gains, support growth, and provide a scalable platform for modernization in the industrial sector.
