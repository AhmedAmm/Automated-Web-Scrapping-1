## Products & Services
The RFP details a comprehensive suite of telecommunications and IT services, categorized as "Service Areas." Each Service Area contains multiple specific "Services."

- **Service Areas:**
    - Data Service (mandatory services include Virtual Private Network Service, Ethernet Transport Service)
    - Voice Service (mandatory services include Internet Protocol Voice Service, Circuit Switched Voice Service)
    - Contact Center Service (optional)
    - Colocated Hosting Service (optional)
    - Cloud Service (optional, including IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, Content Delivery Network Service)
    - Wireless Service (optional)

    - Access Arrangements (mandatory)
    - Service Related Equipment (SRE) (optional)
    - Service Related Labor (optional)
    - Cable and Wiring (optional)
    - National Security and Emergency Preparedness (NS/EP) (mandatory CLINs, not a service)
- **Specific Services and their Pricing Structures (Examples):**
    - **Virtual Private Network Service (VPNS):** Includes Transport Charges, Optional Transport with Embedded Access Charges, and Feature Charges. Pricing depends on sites, bandwidth, security, and access type. Scalable and burstable bandwidth options are available. (mentioned in document)
    - **Ethernet Transport Service (ETS):** Includes Port price, Ethernet Virtual Connection (EVC) price (for E-LINE), and Features. Scalable and burstable bandwidth options are available. (mentioned in document)
    - **Optical Wavelength Service (OWS):** Includes MRC per wavelength for transport and Feature Charges. Pricing varies by mileage band for CONUS, and fixed + per-mile for OCONUS/non-domestic. (mentioned in document)
    - **Private Line Service (PLS):** Includes MRC per circuit for transport and Feature Charges. Pricing can be distance-based or a combination of fixed + distance-based. (mentioned in document)
    - **Synchronous Optical Network Service (SONETS):** Access priced separately; SRE pricing as per Section B.2.10. (mentioned in document)
    - **Dark Fiber Service (DFS):** Includes MRC for IRU (Indefeasible Rights of Use) for on-net connections or along routes. Management, monitoring, and repair are included in MRC. (mentioned in document)
    - **Internet Protocol Service (IPS):** Includes MRC per Port and Feature Charges. Scalable and burstable bandwidth options are available. Embedded and non-embedded access options. (mentioned in document)
    - **Broadband Internet Service (BIS):** Includes NRC, MRC, and Feature Charges. All BIS ports include embedded access. BIS port CLINs are priced as ICB (Individual Case Basis). (mentioned in document)
    - **Voice Service:**
        - **Internet Protocol Voice Service (IPVS):** Includes basic service per telephone number (unlimited on-net to on-net and CONUS off-net calling), per six-second off-net usage, and features. Options include Hosted and Premises-Based IPVS, Managed LAN, and Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) Trunk Service. (mentioned in document)
        - **Circuit Switched Voice Service (CSVS):** Includes usage (switched originating access/terminating access costs per six-second increment), and features. Unlimited CONUS to CONUS or within the same OCONUS country/jurisdiction calling plans are available. (mentioned in document)
        - **Toll-Free Service (TFS):** Includes transport usage (switched originating access/terminating access costs per six-second increment), payphone surcharge, and feature charges. Options include switched, dedicated, or IP termination. Flat monthly rates for transport usage are optional. (mentioned in document)
        - **Circuit Switched Data Service (CSDS):** Priced based on originating access, transport, and features. Transport prices are usage-based in six-second increments. (mentioned in document)
    - **Contact Center Service (CCS):** Includes NRC for service initiation, NRC/MRC based on concurrent agents, and Feature Charges. Pricing structures differ for Host-Based and Premises-Based CCS. (mentioned in document)
    - **Colocated Hosting Service (CHS):** Includes NRC, MRC, usage charges for bandwidth, and Feature Charges. Monthly MRC for rack space includes basic power. Internet usage is determined by the 95th percentile method. (mentioned in document)
    - **Cloud Service:** Requires a catalog pricing approach. Services include IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, and Content Delivery Network Service. (mentioned in document)
- **Pricing Information:**
    - Prices are structured using CLINs (Contract Line Item Numbers). (mentioned in document)
    - Pricing can be fixed or ICB (Individual Case Basis). (mentioned in document)
    - Pricing elements include MRC (Monthly Recurring Charges), NRC (Non-Recurring Charges), and Usage-Based Charges. (mentioned in document)
    - Banding structures are used for pricing based on distance or bandwidth. (mentioned in document)
    - Prices are specified and billed in U.S. currency, with specific decimal place limitations. (mentioned in document)
    - Discounts offered in catalogs shall not vary by geographic location, but TO-specific discount increases are permitted. (mentioned in document)
    - Prices are fixed for each applicable fiscal year. (mentioned in document)
    - Prices exclude the Associated Government Fee (AGF). (mentioned in document)
