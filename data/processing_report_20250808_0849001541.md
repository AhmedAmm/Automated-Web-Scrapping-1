# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a press release detailing Orange's financial results for the first half of 2025, ending June 30th. The company reported solid performance across its key segments, including France, Europe, and Africa & Middle East. Notably, Orange achieved a 3.8% growth in EBITDAaL (EBITDA after Leases) and a 7.7% increase in Organic Cash Flow from telecom activities. The company also announced an upgrade to its full-year 2025 EBITDAaL growth guidance to above 3%.

Key business insights highlight a strong commercial performance in France, with Orange retaining its leading position in mobile network quality. The Africa & Middle East segment demonstrated particularly robust growth, with a double-digit increase in EBITDAaL for the tenth consecutive half, driven by network expansion and the growth of Orange Money and B2B services. Orange Business is strategically focusing on new areas like defense and security. The company also reaffirmed its financial objectives, including a dividend floor of 0.75 euros per share for 2025. The report also touches upon Orange's sustainability commitments, including significant reductions in greenhouse gas emissions and progress in digital inclusion.
