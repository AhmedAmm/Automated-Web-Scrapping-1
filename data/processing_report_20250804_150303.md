# Market Intelligence Summary

## Document Overview
This document is a guide on calculating the Return on Investment (ROI) for Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, emphasizing the importance of this calculation beyond just initial purchase and implementation costs. It highlights that while qualitative benefits like improved staff retention and faster decision-making are significant, quantifying tangible gains such as reduced operating expenses and increased inventory turnover is crucial for justifying the substantial investment in an ERP solution.

The content specifically promotes Acumatica as a provider of tools to assist businesses in this ROI calculation, including an ERP benefits calculator and a free eBook. It features a testimonial from Ethan Platt of American Meadows (AMI), who discusses how Acumatica Cloud ERP has enabled their business to operate at scale while gaining detailed customer insights and meeting consumer expectations. The core message is that a thorough, personalized ROI projection is essential for any company considering an ERP investment, and Acumatica offers resources to facilitate this process.
